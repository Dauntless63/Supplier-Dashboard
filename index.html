ABC-XYZ supplier segmentation and expediting

Suppliers are ranked by annual spend (ABC) and by lead-time variability (XYZ). Each segment gets its own follow-up routine, and open POs are ranked so you chase the ones most likely to be late and most costly first. Dummy data: 100 suppliers, 12 past deliveries each, 260 open POs.

Segment matrix

Click a segment to filter the lists below. A = top 80% of spend, B = next 15%, C = last 5%. X = steady lead times, Y = some variation, Z = erratic. Colour shows on-time delivery of that segment: green 90% or more, amber 80-89%, red below 80%.
